Manual substation patrols were dangerous, and consuming 40 hours a week.

A 330kV substation required twice-daily visual and thermal inspection across 60 checkpoints. Commercial patrol robots on the market weren't built for substation safety protocols, and none integrated with the operator's existing monitoring system.

We built a tracked patrol unit with a stabilized thermal and visual head, routed against the operator's existing checkpoint map.

Patrol labour replaced. Thermal abnormalities detection rate increased by 60%.

Ten years on, we still maintain that unit. It's the reason the data is still worth trusting.
